I am a research scientist working on **Responsible AI**, **AI safety**, and the **evaluation** of large language models and generative AI systems. I am interested in how we measure whether AI systems are safe, fair, and reliable: designing benchmarks and metrics, building trustworthy automated evaluators such as LLM judges, and uncovering emerging risks, from deception and long-context safety to failures that arise when multiple AI agents interact. My goal is evaluation rigorous enough to guide how models are built and deployed. I am currently a Senior Applied Scientist at Amazon, and I received my Ph.D. in Computing and Information Sciences from the Rochester Institute of Technology under [Dr. Linwei Wang](https://www.rit.edu/cblwang/).

I am open to collaborations on AI safety, model evaluation, and trustworthy ML. Reach me at **jwala [dot] dhamala [at] gmail [dot] com**.

Research Interests
======

**Evaluation & Benchmarks.** Rigorous benchmarks, metrics, and automated evaluators (LLM-as-a-judge and rule-based) for measuring safety, fairness, and content safety in LLMs, including multilingual and low-resource settings. [[BOLD](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3442188.3445924)] [[Intrinsic vs Extrinsic Fairness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.08670)] [[Agentic Benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02825)] [[Amazon Nova](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12103)]

**Emerging Risks & Failure Discovery.** Red-teaming, jailbreaks, and adversarial probing to surface failures that standard evaluations miss, including LLM deception, long-context safety, and risks in agentic and multi-agent systems. [[LH-Deception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.03999)] [[DECOR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19270)] [[JAB](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09473)]

**AI for Healthcare.** Reliable, uncertainty-aware ML for high-stakes clinical applications, building on my Ph.D. in probabilistic ML (Bayesian optimization, Gaussian processes, generative models) for personalized cardiac simulation. [[Graph BO-VAE](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.01406)] [[GP-MCMC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.01983)] [[S2S-LSH](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.06106.pdf)]

Earlier, I worked on fairness mitigation for language understanding models using weakly supervised learning, active learning, and data augmentation.
