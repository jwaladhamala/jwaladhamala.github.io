I am a research scientist working on **Responsible AI**, **AI safety**, and the **evaluation** of large language models and generative AI systems. I am interested in how we measure whether AI systems are safe, fair, and reliable: designing benchmarks and metrics, building trustworthy automated evaluators such as LLM judges, and uncovering emerging risks, from deception and long-context safety to failures that arise when multiple AI agents interact. My goal is evaluation rigorous enough to guide how models are built and deployed. <!-- Recent work includes [LH-Deception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.03999) (ICLR 2026), [best practices for agentic benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02825) (NeurIPS 2025), and the [Amazon Nova Technical Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12103) (2025). --> I am currently a Senior Applied Scientist at Amazon, where I recently led Responsible AI quantitative evaluation for Amazon Nova 1.0 and Nova 2.0 (AI Service Cards: [Nova 1.0](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/pdfs/ai/responsible-ai/nova-micro-lite-pro/nova-micro-lite-pro.pdf), [Nova 2 Lite](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ai/responsible-ai/nova-2-lite/overview.html)). I received my Ph.D. in Computing and Information Sciences from the Rochester Institute of Technology in 2020 under [Dr. Linwei Wang](https://www.rit.edu/cblwang/).

I am open to research collaborations, speaking, and advising on AI safety, model evaluation, and trustworthy ML. Reach me at **jwala [dot] dhamala [at] gmail [dot] com**.

Research Interests
======

**Evaluation & Benchmarks.** Rigorous benchmarks, metrics, and automated evaluators (LLM-as-a-judge and rule-based) for measuring safety, fairness, and content safety in LLMs, including multilingual and low-resource settings. [[BOLD, ACM FAccT 2021](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3442188.3445924)] [[Intrinsic vs Extrinsic Fairness, ACL 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.13928)] [[Agentic Benchmarks, NeurIPS D&B 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02825)] [[Amazon Nova, arXiv 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12103)]

**Emerging Risks & Failure Discovery.** Red-teaming, jailbreaks, and adversarial probing to surface failures that standard evaluations miss, including LLM deception, long-context safety, and risks in agentic and multi-agent systems. [[LH-Deception, ICLR 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.03999)] [[DECOR, arXiv 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19270)] [[JAB, arXiv 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09473)]

**Trustworthy AI for Healthcare.** Evaluation, uncertainty quantification, and reliability for AI in high-stakes clinical settings. My Ph.D. developed probabilistic ML (Bayesian optimization, Gaussian processes, generative models) for personalized cardiac simulation, and I am interested in bringing rigorous safety evaluation to clinical and health LLMs. [[Graph BO-VAE, MICCAI 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.01406)] [[GP-MCMC, MedIA 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.01983)] [[S2S-LSH, IEEE Sensors Letters 2018](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.06106.pdf)]

Earlier, I worked on fairness mitigation for language understanding models using weakly supervised learning, active learning, and data augmentation.
